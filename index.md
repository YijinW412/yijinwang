## About

I am Yijin Wang (<span lang="zh-Hans">王仪瑾</span>), a second-year PhD student at American University. I work in the lab of [Prof. Bei Xiao](https://sites.google.com/site/beixiao/home?authuser=0), where we investigate how the human brain perceives materials in different dimensions, using a combination of machine learning and neuroscience methods. Recently we have become interested in how humans memorize different kinds of materials.

I previously worked in the lab of [Prof. Juan Chen](http://juanchenpsy.scnu.edu.cn/), investigating the representation of tools under unconscious conditions using deep neural networks.
